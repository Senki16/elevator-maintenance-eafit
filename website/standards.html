Standards

Safety is regulated.
Down to the last floor.

Two Colombian technical standards (NTC), published by ICONTEC, frame how EAFIT's elevators are built, installed and inspected.

NTC 5926-1ICONTEC · Colombian technical standard

Inspection of elevators, escalators, moving walkways and power-operated doors

Part 1 covers electromechanical and hydraulic elevators and sets the safety and inspection requirements used to verify that an installed elevator conforms.

Periodic inspectionSafety requirementsElectromechanical & hydraulic
  • Defines what an inspector checks and how conformity is determined.
  • Basis for the third-party inspection in every plan of the simulator.
NTC 4349ICONTEC · Colombian technical standard

Safety rules for the construction and installation of lifts

Governs passenger and passenger-and-goods elevators, with particular applications on accessibility to lifts for all persons, including persons with disability.

ConstructionInstallationAccessibility
  • Applies to new installations and to replacements on campus.
  • Accessibility is a constraint on any plan that reduces the number of elevators.

Two definitions
the standards turn on.

Competent person

A person with demonstrated ability to apply knowledge and skills to a given work activity.

Inspection

Examination of a product's design and of the product itself, and determination of its conformity with specific requirements or on the basis of professional judgement.

What the standard does not cover.

Ships

Equipment used to outfit ships and offshore exploration facilities.

Mines

Elevators installed in mines or used to handle radioactive material.

Construction sites

Rack-and-pinion elevators, strip lifts and hoists used on building construction sites.

Going further

A stricter foreign standard.

One of the study's cost routes is to adopt a foreign standard more rigorous than the Colombian one. These are common international references for that route.

Europe

EN 81-20 / EN 81-50

Safety rules for the construction and installation of passenger and goods passenger lifts, and the design rules, calculations and tests behind them.

Europe

EN 81-80

Rules for improving the safety of existing lifts through a structured risk review.

Europe

EN 13015

Maintenance of lifts and escalators: rules for maintenance instructions.

North America · ISO

ASME A17.1 / ISO 25745

The North American safety code for elevators, and the international standard for the energy performance of lifts.

International references are added for context; the study's presentation cites NTC 5926-1 and NTC 4349.
