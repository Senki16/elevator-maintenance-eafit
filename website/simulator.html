    Assumptions

    Illustrative model. The presentation contains no numeric cost or performance data. Every figure here is a placeholder from the repository's cost model (12 elevators, COP 18 M/yr maintenance and 6 M/yr energy per unit, COP 80 M modernisation per unit, 30% energy and 15% maintenance savings, +10% inspection cost for the stricter standard, 2 elevators removed). Maintenance cadences, failure rates and repair times are also assumptions for visualisation. Replace them with real contracts and quotes before drawing conclusions. Reducing the fleet also costs waiting time and accessibility, which the model does not price.
