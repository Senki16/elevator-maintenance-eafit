What is an elevator?

Mechanics, electronics
and hydraulics. In one shaft.

A permanently installed lifting device with a car for people and goods, suspended by cables, chains, pulleys or pistons, whose cables hang less than 15° from vertical.

Motor

The heart of the elevator. It drives the cable that raises and lowers the car.

Car

Where passengers ride, designed for a safe and comfortable trip.

Counterweight

Balances the car and its passengers, cutting the energy the motor must supply.

Sensors

Detect the car's position, passenger movement and other operating factors.

Controller

Turns sensor information into commands that move the car.

User interface

Lets passengers request the floors they want to visit.

Built-in safety

Safety brake if the main system fails, automatic doors, emergency lighting and an alarm button that connects passengers with maintenance staff.
